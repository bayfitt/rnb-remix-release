## Definitions and safe statistical language

- Points: `2 × FGM + 3PM + FTM`. Three-point makes are already included in total field-goal makes; do not count them as three extra points.
- FG% = FGM/FGA; 3P% = 3PM/3PA; eFG% = (FGM + 0.5×3PM)/FGA. eFG adjusts the extra point on a three, but does not include free throws.
- Approximate TS% = PTS / [2×(FGA + 0.44×FTA)]. This is a conventional estimate, not exact possession-by-possession efficiency.
- Assist-to-turnover notation: "eleven assists, zero turnovers" is fine. Do not call it an 11:1 ratio; division by zero is undefined.
- A game's points are not offensive rating. ORtg is points per 100 possessions; the score alone cannot establish possessions or ORtg. Likewise, net rating is not just the scoreboard margin unless the possession denominator is supplied.
- A player plus/minus requires a complete substitution/score timeline; it cannot be inferred from personal scoring or the final margin. Do not invent a plus/minus from these illustrative box scores.
- Regular-season isolation PPP, individual-game FG%, and series averages are distinct denominators. Announce the period when switching between them.
- All new Game 7/Finals numbers below are authored fiction. They are arithmetic-checked creative constraints, not a statistical simulation or researched alternate result.
