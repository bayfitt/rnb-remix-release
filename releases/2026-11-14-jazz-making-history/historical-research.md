## Sports theory evaluation

**Chosen hypothesis:** Utah protects its late Game 6 possession and creates another chance to score instead of losing the ball. This is a plausible single-possession fork, not evidence that Utah would necessarily win either game. A resulting Game 7 would be in Salt Lake City under the then-current 2–3–2 Finals schedule ([NBA format history](https://pr.nba.com/nba-finals-format-change-2014/)). Chicago still has Jordan, Pippen, Rodman and championship experience, so the film must show a contested deciding game rather than an inevitable rout.

**Why this is stronger than a generic superteam rewrite:** Utah already reached the final possession of an elimination game with the lead. Its demonstrated pick-and-roll/post offense and Malone's real Game 5 scoring support a credible imagined response. Do not claim that one secured possession mathematically guarantees a title. Do not present a conspiracy/referee claim as the cause.

**1997 alternative:** If user selects 1997, rewrite the run completely: Clippers 3–0, Lakers 4–1, Rockets 4–2, Bulls actual 2–4. Stockton's famous Houston buzzer-beater belongs to 1997, not 1998. Never insert it into the 1998 script.
